FAQ
What is alpha?

alpha is an upcoming Solana narrative intelligence platform designed to help traders discover emerging memecoin metas and monitor shifts in market attention.

Is alpha available yet?

Not yet. alpha is preparing for its first private beta. Join the waitlist to receive early-access opportunities.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. Joining the waitlist only requires your X handle.

How does the referral system work?

After joining, you receive a unique invitation link. Each valid referral improves your priority in the early-access queue.

Is alpha a trading bot?

No. alpha is designed as a market research and narrative intelligence platform, not an automated trading service.

Does alpha guarantee profitable trades?

No. alpha provides market information and research tools. It does not guarantee trading outcomes.

Not financial advice (NFA)

Nothing on alpha is financial advice. Memecoins are volatile and can go to zero.

THE DETAILS

Good to know.

A few things worth being clear about before joining.

Is Alpha live yet?

Alpha is opening in private beta. Join the waitlist to request access as we bring in the first users.

Is the preview showing live market data?

No. The interface shown above is an illustrative product preview. Live views depend on public provider availability and are accessed inside the terminal.

Does Alpha execute trades or hold funds?

No. Alpha is a market research terminal. It does not execute trades, custody assets or require a wallet to join the waitlist.

Are narrative and scanner results guarantees?

No. Narrative labels are heuristic, token data can be incomplete or delayed, and scanner checks are not a security audit. Always verify a token independently.
