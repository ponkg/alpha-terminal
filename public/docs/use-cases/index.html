Use cases

A few practical ways to use Alpha during market research.

▤
01 / DISCOVER

Follow a narrative

See which tokens are grouped under a theme.

  1. Open the Market Tracker.
  2. Choose a category or detected entity.
  3. Compare the listed tokens and available timeframes.
  4. Open a token to inspect its chart and details.
Search by ticker, name or mint address.
Market Tracker guide ↗
▦
02 / CONTEXT

Read the context

Check selected headlines around a topic or event.

  1. Open the News Feed.
  2. Choose a category or search a name.
  3. Check the publisher and publication time.
  4. Open the original article for the full story.
The Feed uses selected RSS publishers, not X/Twitter.
News Feed guide ↗
⌁
03 / INSPECT

Check a token

Review the public details available for a Solana token.

  1. Open the Coin Scanner.
  2. Enter the token's mint address.
  3. Review liquidity, pool age and holder distribution.
  4. Check mint and freeze authority details.
A missing field is unknown. It is not a clean result.
Coin Scanner guide ↗
⌘
04 / WORKSPACE

Arrange your screen

Keep the tools you use close at hand.

  1. Open a tool with Alt + 1–4.
  2. Drag a window by its title bar to move it.
  3. Use the arrow keys to move through supported controls.
  4. Change shortcuts in the Hotkeys window.
Your custom key bindings are saved in this browser.
Workspace guide ↗
Alpha is a research terminal. It does not execute trades or certify that a token is safe. Verify token details with the original sources.
