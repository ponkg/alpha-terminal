GET STARTED

What Alpha is

Alpha is a Solana market-research terminal. It groups tokens by configured themes, brings selected news feeds together and shows public token and pool details.

Find a themeBrowse categories and detected entities in the Tracker.
Read the contextSearch headlines from selected RSS publishers.
Check the tokenReview available pool, holder and authority details.
Keep your placeUse draggable windows and keyboard shortcuts.
GET STARTED

Access and sign in

  1. Submit your X handle through the public waitlist.
  2. When invited, use the email address supplied to you.
  3. Sign in to open the member terminal.

Joining the waitlist does not require a wallet connection. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

TOOLS / 01

Market Tracker

Choose a category to filter the token list. Search by ticker, name or mint address. Select a row to open its chart and token details.

Categories use configured keyword and entity matches from available token metadata. A token can match more than one category; unmatched tokens may appear under Other / Unclassified.

TOOLS / 02

News Feed

Filter selected RSS stories by category, search headlines and open the original publisher link.

Entity tags are matched from article text. A tag gives context; it does not show that a story caused a token's price move. The Feed is not an X/Twitter feed.

TOOLS / 03

Coin Scanner

Enter a Solana mint address to inspect available token and pool details.

  • Liquidity and pool age
  • Holder distribution and concentration
  • Mint and freeze authority status
  • Trading activity and profile details

Provider coverage varies. Missing data means unknown, not safe.

WORKSPACE

Windows and keyboard controls

Drag a window by its title bar to arrange your workspace. Use the Hotkeys window to review or change supported shortcuts.

Alt + 1–4Open Tracker, Feed, Scanner or Hotkeys.
↑ ↓Move through supported rows and controls.
EnterSelect the focused item.
EscBack out or close the active view.

Custom key bindings are stored in the current browser.

DATA

Data sources

Jupiter Tokens V2Trending-token discovery and ranking intervals.
GeckoTerminalPool discovery, chart history and token data.
Selected RSS publishersHeadlines and links in the News Feed.
Token metadata servicesNames, symbols, images and profile details.

Jupiter provides native 5-minute, 1-hour and 24-hour intervals. The 10-minute and 30-minute changes are sampled estimates based on recent 5-minute data.

DATA

Timeframes

Derived 10-minute and 30-minute changes need enough samples. The rolling sample history resets when the service restarts, so a value may be unavailable until new samples arrive.

REFERENCE

Limits and security

Market providers can be delayed, rate-limited or incomplete. Token labels can be wrong, and Scanner checks are not a full audit.

Alpha is a research tool. It does not execute trades or certify that a token is safe. Verify token addresses and data independently.

Alpha does not need your seed phrase or private key. Do not send either to anyone claiming to represent Alpha.
